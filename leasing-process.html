Protecting Your Property, Simplifying the Experience

Leasing Is a System, Not Just Paperwork

Securing a tenant is just the beginning. Red Door Property Management’s leasing process is designed to protect your investment, ensure legal compliance, and create a smooth, professional experience for both owners and tenants — built around documentation, accountability, and long-term performance.

What Our Leasing Process Includes

From Move-In to Move-Out

Detailed Move-In Inspection

A full checklist documenting every room, high-resolution photos and video, and a walkthrough form signed by the incoming tenant — establishing a clear baseline for the home’s condition. We also include a follow-up video inspection around three months into each lease at no extra cost, to promote tenant accountability and catch small issues early.

Legally Robust Lease Agreement

Custom-built legal documents, not generic templates — prohibiting unauthorized occupants and subletting, enforcing HOA rules, and outlining maintenance, pest control, utility responsibilities, smoking/pet restrictions, and payment and eviction procedures. Leases are regularly updated to reflect Indiana housing law. All leases are digitally signed with payments processed securely online.

Tenant Portal for Payments & Communication

Tenants set up automatic rent payments via ACH, view payment history, submit maintenance requests, and access lease documents online. All tenant communication flows through the Red Door team — we handle lease inquiries, policy reminders, notices, and violations, and hold tenants accountable for HOA violations or negligence.

110-Day Lease Renewal Process

We begin renewal discussions 110 days before the lease ends: a pre-renewal inspection, a market rent analysis, rent recommendations presented to the owner, renewal negotiations with tenants, and a digitally signed updated lease — giving both parties time to plan ahead.

Move-Out Inspection & Deposit Reconciliation

A complete move-out inspection within 24–48 hours — full video and photo documentation, a room-by-room condition review, and comparison to the move-in report — used to determine any security deposit deductions transparently and legally.

Standard Security Deposit Policy

Unless special circumstances apply, Red Door collects one full month’s rent as a security deposit. Higher deposits may be required for low credit scores, limited rental history, negative landlord references, or multiple pets or high-risk pet profiles — protecting against unpaid rent, damage, and lease violations.

Peace of Mind, Built Into Every Lease

Protection, Clarity, and Consistency

With Red Door Property Management, leasing is more than a signed document — it’s a system for protection, clarity, and peace of mind. Whether you’re leasing your first property or managing an entire portfolio, our team handles the details from start to finish so you don’t have to.
