Washington Township Homes for Rent

Washington Township sits in north-central Marion County, bordered by Hamilton County to the north, Lawrence Township to the east, Center Township to the south, and Pike Township to the west. Population grew from 132,049 in 2010 to 138,678 in the 2020 census. It was organized on April 16, 1822 — initially combined with Lawrence Township until the two split in 1826 — and today includes Broad Ripple Village and Castleton Square, Indiana's largest mall.
