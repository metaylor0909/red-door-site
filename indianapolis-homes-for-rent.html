Indianapolis is the seat of Marion County and the core of the metro Red Door serves. Red Door manages rental homes across the full metro, from downtown out through Marion County's nine civil townships and into the surrounding suburbs.

How Renting With Red Door Works

  • Browsing and applying: View details on any listing and apply directly online through Red Door's application flow.
  • Screening criteria: Verifiable income of at least 3x rent, minimum 580 credit score, maximum 55% debt-to-income, no disqualifying convictions, no aggressive dog breeds, plus standard rental-history checks. Full detail on the Tenant Qualification Criteria page.
  • Showings: Self-guided via digital lockbox once an application clears initial screening — no waiting to coordinate a showing time with an agent.
