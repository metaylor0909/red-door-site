Fee Structure Comparison

How Red Door’s Fee Structure Compares

Many property management companies advertise a low monthly rate but add multiple extra fees throughout the leasing and management process. Red Door’s structure is designed to be clear, competitive, and value-driven.

Fee or Service Industry Standard Red Door Structure
Monthly Management A low advertised monthly rate may not include the full operating support owners expect. 9% of monthly collected rent for full-service management. Investor pricing is 8% for owners with 3 or more doors.
New Lease Fee Leasing fees are often equal to one full month’s rent. $995 flat fee when Red Door places a new tenant for full-service management.
Vacancy Fee Vacancy fees may apply even while rent is not being collected. $0 monthly management fee while your property is vacant.
Marketing Marketing, setup, photos, or showing tools may be separate charges. Professional photos, virtual walkthrough video, listing syndication, and self-guided showing technology are included.
Renewal Renewal fees can vary or be unclear until later in the lease cycle. $350 renewal fee when a tenant renewal is completed.
Inspection Inspection processes may be limited, separate, or not clearly documented. Documented inspection process with annual inspection pricing clearly listed above.
Maintenance Coordination Maintenance coordination costs and dispatch process may be difficult to compare upfront. Troubleshooting before dispatch, vendor coordination, emergency response, and clear per-request pricing listed above.
Owner Communication Communication systems and reporting cadence vary widely between management companies. Owner portal access, monthly owner statements, documented systems, and a clear communication process.
