Indianapolis Market Report

Indianapolis is Indiana's capital and largest city — about 911,000 people inside city limits, with Marion County around it at just under a million. It's also the broadest market Red Door tracks: rather than one neighborhood or a small cluster of suburbs, this page blends rental data across all of Marion County's residential ZIP codes, from downtown high-rises to the nine surrounding townships (each of which also has its own dedicated page). Red Door manages rental homes throughout Indianapolis, and this page holds what we're actually seeing.
