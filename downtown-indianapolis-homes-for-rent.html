Downtown Indianapolis Homes for Rent

This page covers rentals in downtown Indianapolis's core — the Mile Square and the neighborhoods immediately around it (ZIP codes 46202, 46204, and 46225) — separate from the wider Indianapolis metro covered on Red Door's main Indianapolis page. It's the densest, most walkable rental market Red Door manages: government and corporate offices, hospitals, and universities draw a renter base looking to be close to work without a car, and the housing stock reflects that.

What to Expect Renting Downtown

  • Location: Covers ZIP codes 46202, 46204, and 46225 — the Mile Square and the immediately surrounding blocks, distinct from the outlying Indianapolis neighborhoods covered on the main Indianapolis page.
  • Housing stock: Dominated by apartments and condos — single-family homes are a small share (98 of 510 listings) of what's available, and units run smaller than anywhere else Red Door manages.
